At a glance

Five states, readable from across the room

The status item answers one question: what does the collector conclude right now? The verdict is the TUI's own (view.Verdict). Shape and color both carry it, so the state still reads in monochrome or to a color-blind eye.

The status item

Shown at 140% here. Each cell stacks the three menu bar modes: Symbol, Count and Words. The count is what needs attention: incidents, warnings or unknown checks. When nothing does, no number appears.

●  clear▲  degraded✖  incidents or down○  incomplete evidence◌  offline: the collector or the engine stopped answering

The status card

The first card of the panel, in every state. It can't be hidden. The bar under the headline counts every configured check: unknown is hatched, never left out of the denominator. When the engine goes quiet, the card turns CACHED and says what the last snapshot said, rather than leaving an old green on screen.

Rules every state follows

never green without evidence

Nothing is inferred

The app shows only what topo sends. It never decides that something is up, down or at fault, and never sets a threshold of its own.

lease + valid_until

Blind is its own state

Each frame carries the publisher's lease and every row's valid_until. Past either deadline the row is withheld, and when the lease lapses the bar turns ◌, even if the last frame said all clear.

color is never alone

Shape, then color

Badges change shape (● ▲ ■✕ ○ ◌) as well as color, so the state still reads in monochrome. Text keeps text colors, and status colors appear only where they mean status.

local ≠ evidence

This Mac is labeled

Local readings come from the Mac itself, not from a check. They carry no health, keep working while offline, and say "local readings" wherever they appear.
